Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

The short version: your manuscripts, recordings and covers never leave your computer. We only keep what we need to sell you a subscription and check your licence: your name, email, licence key, and the computers it's used on.

1. Who is responsible

{{LEGAL_NAME}} (“we”) makes Manuscript Editor & Formatter and runs this website. Contact us about privacy at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.Contact us about privacy through the “Contact” link in your Paddle receipt.

2. Your work stays on your computer

The app runs on your own PC. Editing, formatting, transcription, ghostwriting, cover editing and audiobook processing all happen locally, using open-source tools. Your manuscripts, audio, pictures and fonts are never uploaded to us, and nobody uses them to train AI.

3. What we collect

  • When you buy: Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record, collects your name, email, billing country and payment details to take the payment and charge the right tax. Paddle shares your name, email, country, plan and subscription status with us. We never receive your card details. See Paddle's privacy policy.
  • Your licence: your licence key, your plan and when it renews or ends, and for each computer you activate: its computer code (a random-looking code made from your PC's Windows ID, not your name or files), the app version, and when it last checked in.
  • Emails we send: your licence email and service messages go through our email provider, Resend, which processes your email address to deliver them.
  • The website: our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser) for security for a short time. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. The site remembers your colour theme and billing choice in your own browser only. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • App updates and downloads: the app checks our website for updates and, when you first use some features, downloads open-source language and speech models from Hugging Face. Those servers see your IP address, as with any download.
  • When you contact us: what you send us, so we can reply.

4. Why we use it

To sell and deliver your subscription, send your licence key, check that a licence is valid, prevent misuse (such as a key shared with many people), help you when you ask, and meet legal and tax duties. We don't sell your information.

5. Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run this: Paddle (payments and tax), Vercel (website and licence service hosting), Neon (our licence database), Resend (email) and Google Fonts. They act on our behalf, or under their own privacy policies in Paddle's case as the seller. Some of them process data outside your country, with the safeguards they describe in their policies.

6. How long we keep it

Licence and customer records are kept while your subscription is active and for up to 6 years after, for tax and accounting. Website logs are kept for a short time by our host. You can ask us to delete your information sooner if we don't need to keep it by law.
