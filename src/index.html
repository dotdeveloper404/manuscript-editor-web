Features

An editor and formatter that respects the author

Not a chatbot, and it never rewrites in secret. Every change is a suggestion showing the original, the edit and the reason, and you accept, reject or ignore it.

Reads the whole book

Every check sees every chapter, so a detail in chapter 20 is compared with what you set up in chapter 2: names, dates, eye colours, timelines.

You decide every change

Each suggestion is marked Error or Suggestion, with a severity. Accept, reject, ignore or write your own fix, using the keyboard if you like.

Four editing passes

Structural, Line editing, Copyediting and Proofreading, in the order a professional editor works, so big fixes come before commas.

Your original is never touched

The upload is stored separately and never modified. Edits go into a new revision, and you can always download the original again.

Auto Correct Everything

Accept the safe fixes in one go (spelling, grammar, punctuation, capitals) and see the count first. Names are left alone, and one click undoes it all.

Keeps your Word layout

Accepted edits go straight into your own .docx, so your headings, chapters, styles and front matter stay exactly as you made them.

How it works

From first draft to print-ready book in five steps

  1. 1

    Upload

    Word, text, Markdown or PDF. Chapters are found from your headings, and italics are kept.

  2. 2

    Analyse

    Grammar, style, pacing, dialogue, point of view, tense and continuity, across the whole book.

  3. 3

    Review

    Work through each pass. See the original, the suggestion and why. You choose.

  4. 4

    Apply & re-check

    Accepted edits become a new revision, which is checked again for the next pass.

  5. 5

    Format & export

    Pick a book template and export a print-ready Word book, or the edited text and a corrections report.

Audio to manuscript

Talk your book. We'll type it.

Record a chapter whenever you're ready, on any day, and add it to your audio project. Each recording is transcribed on your own computer with open-source speech models, with no per-minute fees.

  • Clean text automatically. Ums, uhs and stutters are removed, and paragraphs follow your pauses.
  • Say "Chapter three. The return." and it becomes the chapter title.
  • Check before you build. Unclear passages are flagged, and you can play exactly that bit of audio.
  • Optional ghostwriting with a local model turns a spoken transcript into a polished chapter, with invented facts flagged for you.
  • One click to a manuscript that opens in the editor, ready to proofread and format.

Accepts MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC, AAC, WMA and common video files.

Book cover studio

A print-ready cover, sized to the exact millimetre

Enter your page count, paper and trim size. The cover studio works out the spine width, bleed and full wrap using Amazon KDP's own cover rules, fits your artwork to it, and lets you change the words on the cover yourself.

  • Exact KDP sizes for paperback and hardcover: spine, bleed, safe areas and the barcode box, with a printable template.
  • Fit your artwork as one wrap-around picture, or a separate front, back and spine. Your title is never cropped off.
  • Edit the text in your picture. It finds the words on your cover, erases them cleanly and lets you retype them in 20+ fonts, or your own.
  • Checks before you print: text too close to the edge, in the barcode area or on a spine that's too thin, and pictures under 300 dpi.

Audiobook studio

Record your own audiobook, ready for Audible

Read your book aloud, chapter by chapter, with your own microphone. The studio checks every take against your text and masters the sound to the standards Audible's ACX asks for. It's your voice, not a computer's, because ACX doesn't accept computer-generated narration.

  • A script made from your book, split into chapters, with opening and closing credits written for you.
  • Room test before you start, so you know your background noise is quiet enough.
  • Reading check: it listens back and marks missed, changed or repeated words, and cuts out your retakes.
  • One-click mastering for loudness, peaks, noise and silence, all checked against ACX's rules.
  • Download for ACX: one MP3 per chapter, credits, a retail sample and a checklist.

FAQ

Questions authors ask

Will it change my book without asking?

No. Every change is a suggestion you accept, reject or ignore. Even Auto Correct only accepts the safe fixes you choose, and one click undoes it. Your original upload is kept untouched.

Do I need an internet connection or an AI subscription?

No AI fees, ever. Everything runs on your PC. It only goes online the first time you use transcription or ghostwriting, to download the free open-source model once.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

This is a test build that isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows "unknown publisher". Click More info, then Run anyway to install.

Will my Word headings and styles survive?

Yes. Accepted edits are written into your own .docx in place, so chapters, headings, intro pages and styles stay as you made them.

How long does transcription take?

On a typical laptop, a few minutes for a four-minute recording in Balanced quality. You can keep working while recordings wait in the queue.

How does the free trial work?

Download the app and you get every feature for 7 days on one computer, starting the first time you open it. When the 7 days are over, the app asks for a licence code. Your manuscripts stay safely on your computer and are there again as soon as you buy.

Can I get more time on the trial?

Yes. In the app, open Licence (top right), copy your computer code and send it to us. We'll reply with an extension code that unlocks more days on that computer.

How does billing work?

Choose monthly or yearly. Paying yearly costs 10% less than paying month by month. Your plan renews by itself, and you can cancel any time: it then stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Your manuscripts always stay on your computer.

Can I upgrade from Writer to Author Pro later?

Yes. You can move to a bigger plan, or switch between monthly and yearly, at any time.

Can the app read my audiobook aloud for me?

No, and that's on purpose: Audible's ACX only accepts audiobooks read by a person. The audiobook studio helps you, or a narrator you hire, record it well. It checks each chapter against your text, fixes the sound and prepares the files ACX asks for.

Will my cover fit Amazon KDP?

The cover studio uses KDP's own cover rules for paperback and hardcover (spine width from your page count and paper, bleed, safe areas and the barcode box) and checks your cover before you download it. Amazon still reviews every book you upload.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. The test build is Windows only.
