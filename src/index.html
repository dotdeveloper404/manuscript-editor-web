Features

An editor that respects the author

Not a chatbot, and it never rewrites in secret. Every change is a suggestion showing the original, the edit and the reason, and you accept, reject or ignore it.

Reads the whole book

Every check sees every chapter, so a detail in chapter 20 is compared with what you set up in chapter 2: names, dates, eye colours, timelines.

You decide every change

Each suggestion is marked Error or Suggestion, with a severity. Accept, reject, ignore or write your own fix, using the keyboard if you like.

Four editing passes

Structural, Line editing, Copyediting and Proofreading, in the order a professional editor works, so big fixes come before commas.

Your original is never touched

The upload is stored separately and never modified. Edits go into a new revision, and you can always download the original again.

Auto Correct Everything

Accept the safe fixes in one go (spelling, grammar, punctuation, capitals) and see the count first. Names are left alone, and one click undoes it all.

Keeps your Word layout

Accepted edits go straight into your own .docx, so your headings, chapters, styles and front matter stay exactly as you made them.

How it works

From first draft to finished file in five steps

  1. 1

    Upload

    Word, text, Markdown or PDF. Chapters are found from your headings, and italics are kept.

  2. 2

    Analyse

    Grammar, style, pacing, dialogue, point of view, tense and continuity, across the whole book.

  3. 3

    Review

    Work through each pass. See the original, the suggestion and why. You choose.

  4. 4

    Apply & re-check

    Accepted edits become a new revision, which is checked again for the next pass.

  5. 5

    Export

    The edited book (.docx, .txt, .md), a marked-up corrections report, or the full edit log.

Audio to manuscript

Talk your book. We'll type it.

Record a chapter whenever you're ready, on any day, and add it to your audio project. Each recording is transcribed on your own computer with open-source speech models, with no per-minute fees.

  • Clean text automatically. Ums, uhs and stutters are removed, and paragraphs follow your pauses.
  • Say "Chapter three. The return." and it becomes the chapter title.
  • Check before you build. Unclear passages are flagged, and you can play exactly that bit of audio.
  • Optional ghostwriting with a local model turns a spoken transcript into a polished chapter, with invented facts flagged for you.
  • One click to a manuscript that opens in the editor, ready to proofread and format.

Accepts MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC, AAC, WMA and common video files.

FAQ

Questions authors ask

Will it change my book without asking?

No. Every change is a suggestion you accept, reject or ignore. Even Auto Correct only accepts the safe fixes you choose, and one click undoes it. Your original upload is kept untouched.

Do I need an internet connection or an AI subscription?

No subscription, ever. The app runs on your PC. It only goes online the first time you use transcription or ghostwriting, to download the free open-source model once.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

This is a test build that isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows "unknown publisher". Click More info, then Run anyway to install.

Will my Word headings and styles survive?

Yes. Accepted edits are written into your own .docx in place, so chapters, headings, intro pages and styles stay as you made them.

How long does transcription take?

On a typical laptop, a few minutes for a four-minute recording in Balanced quality. You can keep working while recordings wait in the queue.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. The test build is Windows only.
